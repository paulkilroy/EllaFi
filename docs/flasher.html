Linux: can't find the port, or "failed to open serial port"?

Which port is the ESP32? It's /dev/ttyUSB0 or /dev/ttyACM0 — never /dev/ttyS* (those are inactive motherboard ports; ignore them all). To confirm which one, open a terminal (Ctrl+Alt+T) and run:

ls -l /dev/serial/by-id/

That lists only real USB serial adapters by name, each pointing to its ttyUSB/ttyACM. In the browser's port picker, choose that one — not a ttyS entry.

"Failed to open serial port"? Another program grabbed it — usually ModemManager or brltty on ttyACM, or a permissions issue. Paste this, then try again (replace ttyACM0 with your port):

sudo systemctl stop ModemManager brltty 2>/dev/null
sudo chmod a+rw /dev/ttyACM0

Permanent permission fix (so you don't need chmod each time):

sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER

…then log out and back in. Also make sure nothing else has the port open (Arduino IDE serial monitor, another browser tab, screen/minicom). To see what's holding it: sudo fuser -v /dev/ttyACM0
